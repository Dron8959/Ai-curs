"Won't AI just make my kid lazy?"

It's the question parents ask us most. Honest answer: it depends who has the ideas.

If the AI writes the essay, yes. If your kid invents the game, describes the characters, checks what came out and asks for changes, they're doing more thinking than before, not less.

That's how every lesson works here. If you want a course where AI does the homework for the kid, this isn't it.

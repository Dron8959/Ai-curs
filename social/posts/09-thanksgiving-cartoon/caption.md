This Thanksgiving, let your kid make the entertainment.

1. Pick a family story. The year the pie fell. Grandpa's dog.
2. Your kid draws one scene. Their version, not yours.
3. Turn it into a short cartoon, with their own voice in it.
4. Play it on the TV after dinner.

It's one evening together, and it's the thing the whole family will talk about.

Save this for Thanksgiving week.

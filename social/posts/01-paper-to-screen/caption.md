A 6-year-old drew this in the morning. One lesson later it was a cartoon.

No coding. No art skills. Just three steps, side by side with a grown-up:

1. Your kid describes the scene, out loud, in their own words.
2. You both check what came out. Your kid decides what's missing.
3. Ask again. One change at a time, until it matches what they imagined.

That loop is the real skill. The tool will change. Knowing how to say what you want won't.

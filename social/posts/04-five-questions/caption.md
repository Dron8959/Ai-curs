Nearly 9 in 10 American kids aged 9–17 already use AI. Almost half have never talked about it with a parent. (Common Sense Media, 2026)

You don't need to be an expert to start that talk. Five questions are enough:

1. Which AI have you tried?
2. What did you make with it?
3. Has it ever been wrong?
4. What would you never tell it?
5. What should we make together?

The last one matters most. Kids learn AI anyway. The question is whether they learn it alone.

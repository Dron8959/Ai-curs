# Content that does not read as AI

One rule set for social posts and for the YouTube channels. Where the two differ, the line says so.
Rules marked (video) come from creators who work with AI video daily; (ours) are what we learned on our own posts.

## Facts first
- Only real facts. For Make Show Lab: `social/brand/facts.md` and the live site; reviews word for word, with the note that those families got the course free. For a channel: its own fact sheet. No invented stories, numbers or quotes. (ours)
- A fact you do not have gets a `[NEED: ...]` mark and waits for the owner. It is never filled with a guess. (ours)
- Use a concrete number, name or moment instead of "incredible", "unique", "powerful": "about 20 minutes, 2-3 tries a scene", not "fast and easy". (video + ours)

## Idea
- One directing idea per piece: a question, then the paradox (what does not match what you expect), then the answer. On the channels this is the contradiction between what is said and what is shown. (video + ours)
- One concrete moment beats a list. "Your kid asks for ChatGPT" works; "5 safe ways" does not. The list goes into the saved card or the description. (ours, from our outlier-reel swipe file)
- Write 3-5 hook variants and let the owner choose. (video)

## Text and scripts
- Start with the point. No warm-up ("let's figure it out", "in a world where"). First caption line: 12 words or fewer, no hashtag or emoji first. (ours)
- Mix sentence lengths. Three similar sentences in a row means rewrite.
- Banned shapes: "it's not X, it's Y", "not just X, but Y", automatic lists of three, "on one hand / on the other", rhetorical question answered at once, three short fragments in a row.
- Banned words: unlock, unleash, empower, revolutionize, game-changer, cutting-edge, harness, delve, future-proof, "journey" in the ad sense. No long dashes (use a full stop or a comma). (ours: `social/brand/voice.md`)
- One ask at the end, not three. (ours)
- A script for a live speaker is talking points, not a text to read. A script for a synthetic voice is the exact words. (video + ours)
- Read it aloud. Anything the tongue stumbles on gets rewritten.

## Voice
- Decide what the speaker wants from the viewer before recording. (video)
- Synthetic voice: write as spoken, with commas for pauses. Words in capitals are read letter by letter ("RULES" became "R-U-L-S"), so a spoken keyword is written in lower case. A phrase that blurs when spoken ("Comment game" heard as "come in game") is rephrased ("Type game in the comments"). (ours)
- A voice line must fit its scene: about 2.5 words a second at most. If it does not fit, shorten the line or lengthen the scene, never speed the voice up. (ours)
- A cloned voice carries the platform's AI label. A child's face or voice goes into an AI tool only with the parent's explicit consent, and the recording stays out of public repositories. (ours)

## Picture
- Light grain and soft colour. Do not ship a frame that looks straight out of a model. (video)
- One palette, one font set, one camera and lens per project or channel. Colours and fonts are named in the plan, never left to the tool. (video)
- Something always moves. Mix real footage (the cartoon, phone clips, the kid at the table) with drawn graphics. (video + ours)
- Text and logos are set in the edit with our font, never generated. (video)
- Check hands, objects, signs and spelling frame by frame. (ours)
- Labels and stickers never cover faces. (ours, owner 17 Nov)

## Edit and sound
- Cover micro-pauses between cuts; one cut style per piece. (video)
- Few sound effects. One clean music bed beats several layers. Voice sits at least 10 dB over the music. (video + ours)
- Vary shot length; one slightly imperfect live moment is fine. (ours)

## Before handing over
First line states the point · no banned words or shapes · one concrete number or name · every fact in the fact sheet · read aloud · the voice is in the final file and every line is heard word for word (check with a speech model, not by trusting the build log) · voice fits its scene · our colours and fonts · hands and text checked · labels clear of faces · no micro-pauses · sparse effects · AI label where required · a child's likeness only with consent.

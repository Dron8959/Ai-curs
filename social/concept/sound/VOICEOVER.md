# Voice-over: lines and timing

The reels have music and effects but no voice yet. A voice is the owner's call, so nothing is recorded or
generated here. This file is what a voice needs: the line for every scene and the seconds it has.

## Three ways to do it
1. **Anna and Kamilla record it (recommended).** The brand is a mom and a daughter, not a corporation, and
   their real voices say that better than any design. A phone is enough: a quiet room, the phone 15-20 cm
   from the mouth, one voice memo per reel, each line said twice with a breath between. Put the files in
   `social/media/voice/<reel id>.m4a` (any format). They get cut to the scenes, the music dips about 8 dB
   under each line, and the reel is mastered again.
2. **An AI voice.** Possible, but it needs a decision first: which voice, and how it is disclosed. The
   brand's own rule is that an AI presenter is always labelled, and a synthetic voice saying "I'm Anna"
   would not be true. A neutral narrator is the honest version of this option.
3. **No voice.** Text on screen, music and effects. Most people watch the first seconds muted anyway;
   this is what the reels are now.

## Lines
Every fact is the same as in the captions (`social/brand/facts.md`). Aim for 2.5 words a second or fewer;
lines marked (tight) are over 3 and want a brisk read or a trim. The line starts about 0.3 s after its
scene begins.

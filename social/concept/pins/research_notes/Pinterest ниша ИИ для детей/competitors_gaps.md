## 1. Do direct and adjacent competitors use Pinterest, and how?

### Takeaway
The big kids-coding/AI-class brands have Pinterest profiles, but they look secondary: none of the checked homepages links to Pinterest, and the visible content is generic promo (camps, coding projects, Hour of Code certificates). No competitor was found that runs Pinterest around parent-and-child AI creative projects.

### Cited Findings

- CodaKid homepage has no Pinterest link (only share buttons for its referral program) — [codakid.com](https://codakid.com/)

Snippet counts differ between regional mirrors, so no number is reported — [Pinterest: tynker](https://kr.pinterest.com/tynker/), [Pinterest: gotynker](https://www.pinterest.com/gotynker/)
- Tynker homepage header/footer shows no social links at all — [tynker.com](https://www.tynker.com/)

### Inferences
- Competitors treat Pinterest as a low-effort mirror channel (camp promos, coding-project images). Their audience framing is "kid learns to code" or "teacher resources", not "parent and child create together".
- Because their homepages do not even link Pinterest, the platform is not a priority for them; a small brand can compete on search keywords there rather than on brand size.

## 2. Who makes "AI for kids" content that gets pinned, and what works for them?

### Takeaway
The "AI for kids" content that exists is mostly (a) classroom AI-literacy worksheets on TpT/Etsy/Gumroad aimed at teachers, (b) SEO listicles of "ChatGPT prompts for kids" / "AI art prompts for kids" on tool sites, and (c) AI coloring-page generators. Almost none of it is parent-and-child project content from a real family.

### Cited Findings

## 3. Open gaps and the market climate for a new brand (plus Pinterest growth examples)

### Takeaway
Pinterest's own 2026 parenting data shows parents pulling away from screens, and Pinterest users push back on AI-generated imagery. So "AI for kids" on Pinterest is under-served and also sensitive: the opening is for real, parent-led, offline-linked creative projects that use AI as a tool, with honest (non-AI-slop) visuals. No verified case of a kids-edtech course growing via Pinterest with numbers was found.

### Cited Findings
- Pinterest Parenting Trend Report 2026 (published Feb 24, 2026), theme "raising screen-smart kids who seek real-world adventure": "screen free activities" searches +200% YoY, "no phone summer" +340%, "educational activities for kids" +280%, "family trip vision board" +545%; 54% of Pinterest-using parents support screen-time caps (survey) — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/pinterest-publishes-inaugural-parenting-trend-report/813030/), [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/pinterest-releases-parenting-trend-report-for-2026/), [Pinterest newsroom (not fetchable here)](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/parenting-trend-report-2026/). Other coverage also cites life-skills activities +100% and fun physical activities +340% — [Pinterest newsroom via search snippet](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/parenting-trend-report-2026/). Outlets disagree whether there are 6 or 7 trends.
- Pinterest Predicts 2026: Gen Z drives 67% of predicted trends; themes of nonconformity, self-preservation, escapism; no AI-for-kids trend surfaced — [YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2025/12/10/gen-z-is-driving-two-thirds-of-pinterests-predicted-trends-for-2026/), [Pinterest newsroom](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/pinterest-predicts-nonconformity-self-preservation-and-escapism-drive-21-trends-for-2026/)
- April 2025: Pinterest added "AI modified" labels to AI-generated/modified image pins after "AI slop" backlash — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/30/pinterest-launches-new-tools-to-fight-ai-slop). October 2025: users can tap "Show fewer AI Pins" and turn off GenAI per category (beauty, art, fashion, home decor) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/16/pinterest-adds-controls-to-let-you-limit-the-amount-of-ai-slop-in-your-feed/?sidebar=a). Parents can also limit GenAI content for teens (launched Oct 13, per search summary) — [Protect Young Eyes](https://www.protectyoungeyes.com/apps/pinterest-parental-controls-app-review) (UNVERIFIED detail)
- Platform scale: 631M monthly active users as of Q1 2026 (per Hootsuite) — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/pinterest-statistics/)
- Growth examples (none kids-edtech): homeschool blogger says Pinterest was her #1 referrer for years, 35K followers — [Tina's Dynamic Homeschool Plus](https://tinasdynamichomeschoolplus.com/tag/pinterest-2/); Pinfinite Growth student grew Pinterest traffic 33% (course seller's own case study, self-reported) — [Melyssa Griffin](https://www.melyssagriffin.com/pinfinite-growth-review/); Thinkific case: parent-education course creator Amy Lang made $113,000 (Pinterest's role not confirmed) — [Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com/case-studies/birds-bees-success-story/); Teachable guide on Pinterest for courses — [Teachable](https://www.teachable.com/blog/pinterest-marketing-for-online-courses); TpT-seller podcast on using Pinterest for digital products in 2025 — [Teacher Biz Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2183865/episodes/17963783-122-how-to-use-pinterest-to-grow-your-digital-products-business-in-2025-with-nadalie-bardo)

### Inferences (observed gaps, not measured search volume)
1. **Parent + child AI projects, step by step** ("make a video game with your kid using AI", "AI song with your child", "make a sticker pack with AI"): competitors pin coding projects for kids alone; teacher sellers target classrooms. No family-made project brand found on Pinterest.
2. **AI safety / family AI rules for parents** (printable family AI agreement, "is ChatGPT safe for my 8 year old"): classroom versions exist (SAFE AI rules poster, chatbot safety worksheets), parent-at-home versions not seen.
3. **"Screen-smart" AI**: tie AI creation to offline results (print the sticker pack, color the AI coloring page, perform the AI song) to ride the +200% screen-free / +280% educational activities trends instead of fighting them.
4. **Kid-made results, not AI-polished stock**: given AI labels and "show fewer AI pins", pins showing real kid work, the kid's hand, parent and child at the table (without showing faces if preferred) fit the platform better than glossy AI images. Possible caveat: AI-made artwork in a pin may get the "AI modified" label.
5. **Prompt lists with real outcomes**: "AI art prompts for kids" is held by tool sites; a family brand can show prompt + what a 10-year-old actually got.
6. **Gift / grandparent angle** (AI-made storybook or song as a gift from a grandchild): no content observed in searches; unverified as a gap, needs Pinterest search check.
7. **Seasonal hooks**: holiday coloring pages and Halloween/Christmas projects already work for Tynker and coloring tools; a seasonal "make it together with AI" project is a natural entry.

### Gaps
- No real Pinterest search-volume or autocomplete data for any of these intents (needs Pinterest Trends / Guided Search in a browser).
- No verified example of a kids AI/coding course that grew via Pinterest, with numbers.
- Whether "AI for kids" pins are suppressed or labelled by Pinterest's AI filters when they show AI-generated art: not documented.

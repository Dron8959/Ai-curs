The outcome

What your kid walks away with

By the end there is a folder of things they actually made, and a few habits that outlast the course.

  • Directs AI, doesn't poke at it

    Your kid learns to describe what they want, check the result and ask again until it matches what they imagined. That's the skill behind every tool.

  • Finishes things

    Each lesson has a clear finish line: a file saved, a song played, a print on the fridge. Starting and finishing becomes normal.

  • Creative confidence

    A cartoon with their own voice, a song from their words, a game their friends can play. Kids who've made things stop saying "I can't draw."

    The results live on your laptop, not in a workbook. A cartoon to play at Thanksgiving, a game the cousins can try, a website with their name on it.

    Each lesson has a short part for grown-ups. Plenty of parents finish the course knowing the tools better than their coworkers.

Study plan

How one lesson works

Every lesson runs on the same three steps. Dull to describe, surprisingly hard to stop once you start.

  1. 1

    Watch

    A short video shows the finished result first, so your kid knows exactly what they're about to make.

  2. 2

    Make

    Side by side, in one sitting. Your kid has the ideas and gives the directions. You are the hands on the keyboard, and sometimes the voice of reason.

  3. 3

    Show

    They show it to the family — and can send it in for the course showcase.

Q&A

Questions parents ask

When do we get access?

The same day you pay. Your login arrives by email, and access does not expire.

How much of my time does it take?

One lesson is a short video, then 15–20 minutes of making together. Most families finish a lesson in one sitting. There is no schedule — but if you keep a steady rhythm and finish in a couple of weeks, the skills stick better.

Does my child need their own AI accounts?

No. Every tool runs on your account, so you are in the room for all of it and see everything your child makes.

Do we have to pay for AI tools?

The projects are made with ChatGPT, Gemini, Canva, Suno, CapCut, Higgsfield and a few more, all running on your account. Want to know exactly what you'll need before you buy? Write to nova@makeshowlab.com — Anna answers personally.

What's the difference between the two plans?

Both include all 37 lessons, every project, the grown-up parts and lifetime access. The only difference is whether a real person checks your kid's work: on Creator + Homework, Anna personally reviews every piece you send in, the same day.

Won't AI just make my kid lazy?

Not here. Your kid has the ideas and gives the directions — AI is a helper, not a homework machine. They learn to describe what they want, check the result and ask again. If you want a course where the AI does the homework for the kid, this isn't it.

What if it's not for us?

Finish the first lessons. Not right for your family? Full refund — one email within 30 days is enough.

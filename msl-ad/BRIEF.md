# Make Show Lab: ad brief

## Product
Online course for kids 6–14, taken **together with a parent**. Kids learn to create with AI:
images, video, animation, voice, games and websites. Every lesson follows the loop
**Watch → Make → Show** and ends with a finished piece the kid is proud of.

## Audience
English-speaking parents in the US, mostly moms.
Fears: screen time; AI doing the thinking for the kid.

## Core idea
Not one more passive screen: we turn the habit of scrolling Shorts into the skill of creating
your own content, and the kid and parent do it together.

## Format
Instagram Reels / Shorts ad, 9:16, 1080×1920. Language: English.

## Style reference
`refs/ref1_IMG_7173.MP4`: an explainer Reel (not ours) with a single continuous motion-graphics
animation over a fast voiceover. Borrow the techniques, not the look:
- off-white background with a faint grid; one strong accent colour + small secondary accents
- word-by-word captions on top: active word bold black, previous words grey, key terms in a
  "selected object" box
- left vertical step timeline with a moving avatar marker and numbered step chips
- UI mock cards with soft shadows, pop-in with slight overshoot, lines drawing on, counters
- ending: checklist recap + profile/CTA card
Do **not** reuse the author's cartoon avatar or exact layout.

## Open questions
- Voiceover: who records it (English)?
- Length: 15 / 30 / 45 s?
- Brand: logo, colours, fonts, mascot?
- Real material: kids' finished works, footage of kid + parent?
- CTA and offer (free trial lesson? link in bio? price?)
